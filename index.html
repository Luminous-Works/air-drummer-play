Air Drummer

A hands-only AR drum game. Sit on your couch or in a seat, pinch a drumstick out of the air, and play along with Professor Beat Bop.

Start

  • On a Meta Quest: open this page in the Quest Browser, make sure hand tracking is on, and press Enter XR. The game appears in your room.
  • On a computer: press Enter XR (click again if nothing happens). A virtual headset in a virtual kitchen opens, with your hands set up as tracked hands. Use the panels at the bottom, or drag the colored handles, to move a hand and set its Pinch to 1 to grab a stick.
  • Sit down. After about a second the kit settles at lap height, the snare between your knees.

Play

  • Pinch your thumb and index finger together to hold a drumstick. Swing it down onto a pad to hit it.
  • Colored notes fall onto the pads. Hit a pad as its note lands. A short lesson from the Professor starts you off.
  • The ROCK gauge rises when you play well and drops when you miss; the crowd reacts to it.
  • Finish songs to earn stars and XP. Levels unlock stage decorations.

Controls

  • Menu: pinch both hands together for a moment. Swipe left or right to change pages: stages, extra drums, percussion, kit settings, progress. Tap a stage; tap it again to switch between its A and B tracks.
  • Transport bar (at cymbal height): back 5 seconds, pause, forward 5 seconds, EDIT. The row under it, with − and +, slows or speeds the song for practice.
  • Thumb flicks (curl your index finger): tap to pause, swipe left or right to jump 5 seconds, swipe forward or back to change the tempo.
  • EDIT: floating instruments appear. Pinch one to pull it out, drop it on a pad to swap it in, use both hands to resize.
  • Kit settings: FIT TO ME re-measures your seat, COMPACT shrinks the kit for tight spaces, LEFT-HANDED mirrors it.

Hidden

Three golden records are hiding in your space. Look straight at one for a moment to find it; a soft chime guides you when you are close. Each gives bonus XP and 20 seconds of Star Power: double score, gold notes and a crowd gone wild.
