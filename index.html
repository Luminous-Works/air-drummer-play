Air Drummer

A hands-only AR drum game. Sit on your couch or in a seat, pinch a drumstick out of the air, and play along with Professor Beat Bop.

Start

  • On a Meta Quest: open this page in the Quest Browser, make sure hand tracking is on, and press Enter XR. The game appears in your room.
  • On a computer: press Enter XR (click again if nothing happens). A virtual headset in a virtual kitchen opens. You do not need a webcam or a headset: play with the keyboard. The keys are two rows laid out like the kit, left to right: R  T  Y  U   crash, tom 1, tom 2, ride
    F  G  H  J   hi-hat, snare (G or H), floor tom     Space pauses, the arrow keys turn the view (left/right turn, up/down tilt), scroll zooms, the - and + buttons on the bar change the tempo, Enter skips the first lesson. A letter floats over each pad. You can also click a pad with the mouse (a stick follows your pointer), press Q to look at the kit from overhead, the side and the front, drag with the right mouse button to look around, press E to show or hide the emulator's hand handles, press B for the big kit (extra cymbals, a third tom, a cowbell and wind chimes), press W to switch the venue (the dark club opens by itself for Disco Fever; press again for the club always, the recording studio, the living room, then your own room), and press O for the menu to change stage or add and swap pieces (click the cards; K jumps to the pieces page; Esc closes it and leaves EDIT). In EDIT, drag a pad or piece with the mouse, Shift-scroll to resize it, Shift-drag to raise or lower it, click + PARTS to open the parts drawer (drag a part onto a pad to swap it in, drop a piece on the drawer or press Delete to remove it), and use the < and > on the bar to switch kits (Rock, Jazz, Mini, Blank, 808). Notes fall toward each pad: hit the pad as the note reaches it (within 0.05 s is Perfect, 0.12 s is Good).
  • Sit down. After about a second the kit settles at lap height, the snare between your knees.

Play

  • Headset: pinch your thumb and index finger together to hold a drumstick, and swing it down onto a pad to hit it.
  • Colored notes fall onto the pads. Hit a pad as its note lands. A short lesson from the Professor starts you off.
  • The ROCK gauge rises when you play well and drops when you miss; the crowd reacts to it.
  • Finish songs to earn stars and XP. Levels unlock stage decorations.

Controls

  • Menu: pinch both hands together for a moment. Swipe left or right (drag with the mouse) to change pages: songs, extra drums, percussion, kit settings, progress, skins, venues and the stick shop. Finishing songs, levelling up and finding golden records earns coins to spend there. Tap a stage; tap it again to switch between its A and B tracks.
  • Transport bar (at cymbal height): back 5 seconds, pause, forward 5 seconds, EDIT. The row under it, with − and +, slows or speeds the song for practice.
  • Thumb flicks (curl your index finger): tap to pause, swipe left or right to jump 5 seconds, swipe forward or back to change the tempo.
  • EDIT: floating instruments appear. Pinch one to pull it out, drop it on a pad to swap it in, use both hands to resize.
  • Kit settings: FIT TO ME re-measures your seat, COMPACT shrinks the kit for tight spaces, LEFT-HANDED mirrors it.

Hidden

Three golden records are hiding in your space. Look straight at one for a moment to find it; a soft chime guides you when you are close. Each gives bonus XP and 20 seconds of Star Power: double score, gold notes and a crowd gone wild.
