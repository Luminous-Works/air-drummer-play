# Air Drummer (play build)

A hands-only, seated AR drum game for Meta Quest, built with the Immersive Web SDK.
Play it: https://luminous-works.github.io/air-drummer-play/

This repository holds only the built website that GitHub Pages serves.

## How to play

**On a Meta Quest:** open the link in the Quest Browser, turn hand tracking on, press **Enter XR**, sit down,
and **pinch** your thumb and index finger to pull a drumstick out of the air. Swing it down onto a pad to play it.

**On a computer** (no headset or webcam needed): press **Enter XR** (click again if nothing happens). A virtual headset
opens in a virtual kitchen. Play with the keyboard, two rows laid out like the kit, left to right:

```
R  T  Y  U     crash, tom 1, tom 2, ride
F  G  H  J     hi-hat, snare (G or H), floor tom
```

**Space** pauses, the **left and right arrows** change the stage, the **up and down arrows** change the tempo, and
**Enter** skips the first lesson. A letter floats over each pad. You can also **click a pad with the mouse** (a stick follows the pointer), press **Q** to see the kit from overhead, the side and the front, and press **B** for the big kit (splash, second crash, china, a third tom, cowbell and wind chimes). In a headset the same switch is on the menu's settings page.

- Colored notes fall onto the pads; hit a pad as its note lands. Professor Beat Bop teaches a short first lesson.
- The ROCK gauge rises when you play well and drops when you miss; the crowd reacts to it.
- Finish songs to earn stars and XP; levels unlock stage decorations.
- **Menu:** pinch both hands together. Swipe left and right to change pages. Tap a stage; tap it again for its B track.
- **Transport bar** (at cymbal height): back 5 seconds, pause, forward 5 seconds, EDIT, and a tempo row (minus and plus).
- **Thumb flicks** on a curled finger: tap to pause, swipe left or right to jump 5 seconds, swipe forward or back to change the tempo.
- **EDIT:** pinch floating instruments out of the air and drop them on a pad to swap them in.
- Three **golden records** are hidden in your space. Look straight at one to find it for bonus XP and Star Power.

A "How to play" button at the top left of the page shows the same help.
